Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how Valid collects, uses, and protects information when you use our app, website, and related services.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does Valid buy or sell data?

No. We do not buy or sell any data. Nor will we ever.

I got a text before I joined Valid. How?

Someone may have provided your number or taken an action on Valid that contributed to an invitation. Valid may also send messages associated with its service.

Receiving a text does not mean a Valid account has been created for you.

I don't want to be associated with Valid. How can I delete my information?

Email support@validapp.lol and ask us to delete your information and suppress your phone number. We're happy to help, whether or not you've joined the app. We'll delete your information, subject to the retention requirements below, and suppress your number so friends cannot vote for it on Valid. We retain a limited suppression record to honor your request and prevent your number from being added back for voting.

Information We Collect

  • Account information, such as your phone number, name, age, school, grade, username, and profile details.
  • Contact information you choose to share so we can help you find classmates, invite friends, and power social features.
  • Poll, vote, invite, reveal, subscription, purchase, and app activity information.
  • Content and activity you share, including messages, photos, videos, voice notes, posts, and Stories.
  • When a parent approval or Parent access flow is used, information submitted for that flow, such as a parent or guardian phone number, verification status, and an approval photo.
  • Device, log, analytics, diagnostic, and notification token information used to operate and improve the service.

How We Use Information

  • To create and secure your account.
  • To run polls, invites, reveals, notifications, subscriptions, and other app features.
  • To personalize your experience based on your school, contacts, and app activity.
  • To prevent spam, abuse, fraud, and unsafe behavior.
  • To troubleshoot, analyze performance, and improve Valid.

Contacts

If you grant contact access, we use shared contact information to help identify people you may know, show relevant classmates and suggestions, and support invite flows. If two users have each other’s numbers and sync contacts, they may become friends automatically. Suggestions can reflect contacts, school connections, mutual friends, or another person having saved your number. You can change contact permissions in your device settings; contact support to ask about deleting information already shared.

Friends, Followers, Messages, and Audience Choices

When available, you can choose who may add you, message you, and see your posts and Stories. These are separate settings. A School & contacts setting may include someone who saved your number even if you did not save theirs; the message setting can also consider shared friends and previous conversations. Choices can depend on your age, account, and app version.

Following a public account subscribes you to its public posts; a mutual follow is a friendship. Messages and invitations can be subject to safety checks, limits, or acceptance by the other person. Your profile and content may be visible to other users according to the applicable audience, and recipients can save or screenshot what they see. You can report or block an account in the app.

Parent Approval and Parent Access

For users under 13, the signup flow may ask a parent or guardian to submit an approval photo and may offer a phone number for Parent access. We use the photo to evaluate the approval request and delete it after the check; if deletion fails, cleanup may retry. Approval checks do not establish verified identity or age.

When a parent account is linked, the parent may view a child's text chats, polls, and eligible feed content, and may adjust available privacy controls. The parent view does not show chat photos, videos, or voice notes. Linking requires steps on the child's account and verification of the parent's phone number. A parent or guardian can contact us to ask about access, review, or deletion of a child's information.

Sharing

We do not buy or sell any data. We may share information with service providers that help us host the service, verify phone numbers, process purchases, send notifications, store data, measure app performance, and keep the service secure. We may also share information when required by law or to protect our users, rights, and service.

Subscriptions And Purchases

Purchases and subscriptions are processed by Apple or Stripe, depending on where you purchase. Valid does not receive your full payment-card number. We receive transaction, customer, subscription, and entitlement information needed to unlock paid features, prevent duplicate purchases, validate billing status, and provide customer support.

Data Retention

We keep information for as long as needed to provide the service, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, prevent abuse, and enforce our agreements. You may request account deletion from within the app where available.

Children

Valid offers an approval process for certain users under 13, subject to availability. If you are a parent or guardian and have questions about a child’s information, or believe a child has provided information without the required approval, contact us so we can review and take appropriate action.

Security

We use technical and organizational safeguards designed to protect information, but no online service can guarantee absolute security.

Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
