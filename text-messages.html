Why did I receive a text from Valid?

Texts about Valid can include an invitation shared by someone who knows you or an activity notification sent by Valid. You can receive a message before creating an account. A text by itself does not mean you have joined Valid.

Is Valid affiliated with Peek: Secret Compliment?

No. Valid — Compliment Classmates is not affiliated with Peek: Secret Compliment. They are separate apps. Installing Valid or granting contact access does not automatically send invitations to everyone in your phone book using your number. Members choose whether to send invitations through their phone’s message composer.

We’re clarifying this because KNWA/FOX24’s coverage of compliment texts identifies Peek and quotes a school superintendent’s concerns about messages spreading through users’ contact lists. That reporting concerns Peek. The sections below explain Valid’s invitations, activity notifications, and opt-out options. Our parent FAQ explains our safety practices and privacy commitments.

Invitations and activity notifications

Members can share invitations using their phone’s message composer. For example, after voting for a contact who has not joined, a member may see an Invite prompt and choose whether to send an invitation.

Valid can also send SMS activity notifications through its messaging service. These are separate from messages a member chooses to send from their own phone. The exact reason for a particular message depends on its type and the activity associated with it.

How can I be included before joining?

Members can select people saved in their contacts in polls, including people who have not created a Valid account. A contact match or a vote is not an account registration.

If you do not want to be included, you can ask support to remove your information and suppress your phone number from voting, whether or not you have joined.

How do I stop messages?

For texts sent by Valid’s messaging service, reply STOP when that option is provided. If an invitation came directly from someone’s personal number, ask that sender to stop or use your phone’s blocking controls.

You can also email support@validapp.lol. Tell us if you want texts stopped, your information removed, and your number excluded from voting. These are separate requests; you do not need to install the app to ask for help.

Need help with a specific text?

Contact support with the message and the number that received it so we can investigate. Share those details privately with support rather than posting personal information publicly.
